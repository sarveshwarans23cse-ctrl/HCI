✦ Methodology

My Design Process

A structured, research-driven workflow that transforms user insights into polished, high-fidelity design solutions.

Step 01

Research & Discovery

Conduct user interviews, competitive analysis, and market research to deeply understand user needs, pain points, and business goals.

🔍
👤
Step 02

User Persona

Create detailed user personas based on research data to represent target audience segments and guide design decisions empathetically.

Step 03

User Journey Mapping

Map complete user journeys to identify touchpoints, emotions, and opportunities for improving the overall user experience.

🗺️
✏️
Step 04

Wireframing

Rapidly sketch low-fidelity wireframes to explore layout ideas, information architecture, and core user flows without visual distraction.

Step 05

Low-Fidelity Prototype

Build interactive low-fidelity prototypes to test core user flows early and gather quick feedback before investing in visual design.

📐
🎨
Step 06

High-Fidelity Prototype

Craft pixel-perfect, interactive Figma prototypes with a complete design system, components, variants, and realistic micro-interactions.

Step 07

Usability Testing

Conduct moderated and unmoderated usability tests, analyze task completion rates, identify friction points, and iterate rapidly.

🧪
🏆
Step 08

Final Design & Handoff

Deliver developer-ready design specs, assets, and documentation ensuring seamless transition from design to implementation.
